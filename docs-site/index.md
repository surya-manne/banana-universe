## Why BananaJS

**Not a thin wrapper around Express** — a deliberate stack for teams who want **structure**, **automation**, and a path to **domain-driven design** without adopting a heavyweight runtime. The framework invests in **CLI and AI** so you spend time on product logic, not repetitive files. **Plugins** let the ecosystem grow without bloating core.
